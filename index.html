5 · Findings

Key differences and possible explanations

Differences come from the data; factors are hypotheses to verify with GUS and further Eurostat indicators

Key differences

    Factors behind the gap hypotheses

    1. Skills & awareness: DK/FI non-users cite missing expertise (20–23%); PL ones almost nothing.
    2. Firm-size structure: biggest gap in small firms, which dominate PL. Verify: GUS firm-size data.
    3. Digital foundations: lower data/IT maturity (cloud, ERP). Verify: Eurostat isoc_eb_*.
    4. Sector mix & investment: industry-heavy, lower R&D. Verify: GUS sector, R&D.
    6 · Wrap-up

    Method, limits and next steps

     

    Method & limits

    • Eurostat isoc_eb_ai, share of enterprises, NACE C10–S95 excl. K; barriers as share of non-users
    • Self-reported survey, 4 time points, no sector or regional split
    • 2021 uses an older definition, so the trend is read from 2023
    • Correlation ≠ causation: factors are hypotheses

    Next steps

    • Add GUS "ICT in enterprises" tables to test the factor hypotheses (firm-size structure, sector mix)
    • Add Eurostat digital-maturity indicators (cloud, ERP, e-commerce)
    • Extend with sector breakdown to see where the gap is largest
