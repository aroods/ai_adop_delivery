6 · Findings & recommendations

Goal achieved: the gap is quantified and explained

Differences come from Eurostat, explanations were tested with GUS data. The four recommendations are our interpretation, not proven causal effects.

Key differences

    Recommendations

    1. Awareness first: pilots and demos matter more than subsidies.
    2. Build the base: BI, ERP and advanced cloud lag, and AI sits on top of them.
    3. Target small and medium firms: ratio to Denmark is 6.5× and 3.9×.
    4. Start with generative text AI: the common entry point.
